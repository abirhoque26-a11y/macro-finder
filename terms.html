Accuracy of figures

Calorie, protein, carbohydrate, fat and price figures are estimates. Carbohydrate and fat values are derived arithmetically from the remaining calories and the recipe's main carbohydrate source, not measured. Prices are approximate per-serving costs at UK supermarkets and change constantly. Targets suggested by our calculator come from the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, which is a population-level estimate and will not be right for everyone.

Treat all of these as a guide and adjust based on your own results. We do not guarantee that any figure is accurate for a particular product, portion or person.
