Cottage Cheese & Berries

A UK snack recipe with 290 kcal and 22g of protein per serving, costing roughly £1.30 to make.

vegetariangluten-freebeginnerMeal-prep friendly
290kcal
22gProtein
8gCarbs (est.)
19gFat (est.)
£1.30Per serving

Ingredients

  • cottage cheese200g
  • blueberries60g
  • honey1 tsp

Method

  1. Spoon the cottage cheese into a bowl.
  2. Top with blueberries.
  3. Drizzle with honey and serve.
Meal-prep note

Keep the cottage cheese and berries separate in the fridge and combine when you want it — both keep for a week.
