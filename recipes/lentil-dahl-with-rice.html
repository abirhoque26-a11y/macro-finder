Lentil Dahl with Rice

A UK lunch recipe with 700 kcal and 32g of protein per serving, costing roughly £1.70 to make.

Ingredients

  • red lentils100g
  • basmati rice75g, uncooked
  • light coconut milk150ml
  • turmeric1 tsp
  • garlic2 cloves, minced

Method

  1. Rinse the lentils, then simmer with turmeric and garlic in water until soft, about 15 minutes.
  2. Stir in the coconut milk and simmer a further 5 minutes until creamy.
  3. Meanwhile, cook the rice according to packet instructions.
  4. Serve the dahl over the rice.
Meal-prep note

Freezes exceptionally well — make a double batch and freeze half in portions for a genuinely ready meal later.
