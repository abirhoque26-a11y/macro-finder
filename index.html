About the numbers

where our figures come from ▾
  • Calories & protein are set per recipe from standard UK nutritional data for the listed ingredients and quantities.
  • Carbs & fat are estimated from the remaining calories and the recipe's main carb source — a close guide, not a lab measurement.
  • Ingredient weights are raw/uncooked unless the ingredient itself says otherwise (e.g. "cooked rice").
  • Serving sizes are one serving per recipe, automatically scaled up or down to match your calorie target (never below half or above triple the base portion).
  • Prices are rough per-serving estimates for UK supermarkets, reviewed periodically — they vary by store, brand and season, so treat the weekly total as a ballpark.
  • The supermarket comparison adjusts each recipe's cost using typical UK price differences for the categories of ingredient it contains — so a produce-heavy week and a protein-powder-heavy week rank the stores differently. It is a guide to relative cost between chains, not live pricing, and it can't account for individual promotions or whether a particular store stocks every item.
  • Suggested targets from your weight are generic starting points with a floor of 1400 kcal/day — they are not personal advice.

Macro Mastery is a planning tool, not medical or dietetic advice. If you have a medical condition, a history of disordered eating, or significant weight-change goals, please speak to a doctor or registered dietitian before changing your diet.
